Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This policy covers the Learn Sylheti Language app (the "App") by Md Shahariar Nazim Joy (the "Developer"). It applies to the Android version distributed through Google Play and the iOS version distributed through the Apple App Store.

Summary

The App does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting services.

Data stored on your device

To remember your learning progress, the App saves a small amount of information on your device only: which words you have practised and how well you know them, your study streak, whether you have finished the introduction, and whether you own the lifetime purchase. This information never leaves your device. You can clear it from Settings → Reset Learning Progress, or by deleting the App.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is processed entirely by Google through Google Play Billing on Android, and by Apple through the App Store on iOS. The Developer never receives your name, email address or payment details. Those providers handle purchase data under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Network use

All lessons, audio and documents are bundled inside the App. The App uses the network only to talk to the store's billing service and, if you tap a link, to open a web page or your email app outside the App.

Pronunciation audio

The pronunciation clips were generated ahead of time with an AI voice and are bundled with the App. Nothing you do in the App is sent to any AI or voice service.

Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the Developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. Data on your device is under your control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
